# 02 · Zomato Restaurant Market Expansion

### International Market Analysis

**Microsoft Excel · Pivot Tables · Data Cleaning · Business Intelligence · Market Analysis**

A data-driven analysis designed to identify attractive international markets for restaurant expansion.

### 📊 Dataset

- **9,500+ restaurants**
- **15 countries**
- Multiple cities
- Customer ratings
- Customer votes
- Spending behavior
- Price range
- Online delivery
- Cuisine information

### 🔍 Analysis Areas

**Competition**  
Country-level restaurant concentration and market presence.

**Customer Satisfaction**  
Average ratings and customer engagement.

**Spending Potential**  
Average customer expenditure and premium-market opportunities.

**City-Level Demand**  
Identification of cities showing stronger customer activity.

**Cuisine Performance**  
Evaluation of cuisine categories and customer preferences.

**Restaurant Growth**  
Analysis of restaurant opening trends over time.

### 🎯 Business Question

> **Where should a restaurant network expand, and what type of market positioning should be used?**

### 💡 Key Direction

The analysis identified:

🇮🇩 **Indonesia** → scalable expansion opportunity  
🇵🇭 **Philippines** → strong customer satisfaction and engagement  
🇶🇦 **Qatar** → premium dining opportunity  
🇸🇬 **Singapore** → high-spending premium market  
🇨🇦 **Canada** → low-competition opportunity requiring further validation
